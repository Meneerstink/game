'''Blood money''' is received as a drop in [[Deadman Mode]] after killing another player. It can be used on [[Nigel]] in order to purchase [[Deadman armour]] at the price of {{Currency|Blood money|1}} per piece.

Blood money was also used in the [[Deadman: All Stars Season 3#Blood money|Deadman: All Stars Season 3]] event, where it could be earned in varying amounts for kills and spent on items for the finale.
