The '''fire ruby''' is an [[elemental gems|elemental gem]] obtained by killing [[Infernal Mage|infernal mage]]s. It is used in the creation of the [[amulet of fire]]; the fire ruby must be used on the [[Fire Altar]] along with an [[amulet of magic]] and 100 [[fire rune]]s.

The player may bring a fire ruby or other elemental gems to [[Archmage Sedridor]] to get some hints on what to do with them.
