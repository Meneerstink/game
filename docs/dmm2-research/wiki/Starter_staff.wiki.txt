The '''starter staff''' is a [[powered staff]] obtained from a [[Deadman starter pack]], along with the [[starter sword]] and [[starter bow|bow]]. It has no requirements to wield, and is intended to be used to help a player get started in a seasonal [[Deadman Mode]] server.

It is equipped with a built-in [[Fire Strike]], being able to cast 500 of them before fully degrading, displaying the message {{mes|Your starter staff has fallen apart!|colour=red}}

As with all starter weapons, it cannot be used against other players.
