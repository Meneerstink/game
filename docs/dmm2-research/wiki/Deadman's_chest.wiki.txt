The '''Deadman's chest''' is a cosmetic platebody that can be bought from [[Justine's stuff for the Last Shopper Standing]] for 160 [[Last Man Standing]] points. It is part of the [[Deadman armour]] set.

In [[Deadman Mode]], it has similar defensive stats to an [[iron platebody]] but with no negative offensive stats, and is purchased from [[Nigel]] for one [[blood money]], and may be reclaimed for free. In order to purchase [[deadman armour]] in Deadman mode, players must use the blood money on Nigel, instead of speaking to him, as there are no dialogue options for purchasing the armour.
