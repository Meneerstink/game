'''Annihilation exit portal blueprints''' are received when first using the [[Annihilation blueprints]] to redecorate a [[player-owned house]] to the [[Wilderness (theme)|wilderness theme]] house style. They are used to construct an [[Exit portal#Wilderness|Annihilation exit portal]], which can be used with any [[house style]].

To use the blueprints, players must build an Annihilation [[exit portal]] in the [[centrepiece space]] of a [[garden]] or [[formal garden]] in their [[player-owned house]], requiring 10 [[iron bar]]s and the Annihilation exit portal blueprints. The player must have a [[hammer]] and [[saw]] in their inventory to build it. If players only have one exit portal in their house, they will not be able to remove it until a second one is built.

Players will not receive back the blueprints upon removing the portal. However, additional copies can be obtained by speaking to [[Nigel]] in [[Lumbridge]].
