'''Financial Wizards''' are NPCs that only appear in [[Deadman Mode]] and are located in or near banks. Players can give them ten individual items that they wish to keep safe; when the 10 items are given to the Financial Wizards, they are securely stored in the Safe Deposit Box and cannot be used nor lost on death.

Safe deposit-box can not be accessed while [[Skull (status)|skulled]].

[[File:Deadman_mode_-_Safe_Deposit_Box.png|thumb|left|The Safe Deposit Box, accessed by visiting a Financial Wizard.]]
You cannot store untradeable items. Stackable items such as bank notes, runes, arrows, bolts, etc. will not count as one item. For example, 10 [[air rune]]s will count as 10 items.
