'''Deadman Mode''' (commonly abbreviated as '''DMM''') is a variant of ''[[Old School RuneScape]]'' that released on [[29 October]] [[2015]]. A separate server is hosted featuring an open [[PvP]] environment, with some exceptions. Players in-game can speak to [[Nigel]] in [[Lumbridge Graveyard]] for information about Deadman Mode.

Experience gained in permanent Deadman Mode is 5x to 20x more than usual depending on the level and protection status. Experience is capped to 2x in most [[instance]]d areas (such as the [[TzHaar Fight Cave]]).

Every player begins their adventure at [[combat level]] 3, and progress in ''[[Old School RuneScape]]'' will not be affected. All content is only available to [[members]].

Upon dying to another player, the victim loses everything they had on them. The [[blood money]] currency is gained by killing other players. One is received per kill, and can be used to purchase [[Deadman armour]] from Nigel in the Lumbridge Graveyard. It has the same bonuses as [[iron armour]] and can be reclaimed for free should the player die.

==Variants==
===Seasonal===
In the past, [[Jagex]] would periodically open a brand new Deadman Seasonal server, in which the top 2,000 players were eligible to participate in the Deadman Invitational. The last seasonal Invitational was held in 2020. Invitational rules varied, but generally involved invited participants on fresh accounts with under a week to build their accounts for the finale. During the finale, fog spread throughout most of the world, with a small safe zone, forcing players to fight to stay in the safe zone. The final survivor won a $20,000 cash prize.

Up until season 5, the entirety of the finales would take place in the open world, with the last person standing being the winner. This changed with season 6, which introduced the [[Deadman Isles]] for the finale, splitting up the final 200 players into 4 groups. Season 7 and onward expanded further on this, placing players into [[Deadman Arenas|arenas]] where all fights were 1v1s.

Deadman Seasons and the Invitational were replaced by named Deadman modes running roughly annually.

==== Reborn ====
{{main|Deadman: Reborn}}
[[Deadman: Reborn]] was available from [[25 August]] [[2021]] to [[16 September]] [[2021]]. Major changes from past Deadman Modes included [[Deadman: Reborn/Sigils|Sigils]], which functioned similarly to [[Trailblazer League (2020)#Relics|relics]] from the [[Trailblazer League (2020)|Trailblazer League]], as well as [[combat level]]-separated worlds and experience rate modifiers.

The original finale was held on [[18 September]] [[2021]] and involved the 2048 top players fighting in 1v1 battles, with winners moving on to the next round. The winner of this finale was ''Wolfff90''. Due to extreme network attacks during the finale, the finale was rerun on [[24 June]] [[2022]], with the winner being ''Pid Grabber''. The rerun finale was also plagued by network attacks.

==== Apocalypse ====
{{main|Deadman: Apocalypse}}
[[Deadman: Apocalypse]] was available from [[25 August]] [[2023]] to [[15 September]] [[2023]]. This variant focused on "breaches" in which players had to contain a swarm of monsters that appeared in random locations at regular intervals throughout the day.

The finale was held on [[16 September]] [[2023]] on five finale worlds, with no combat level restrictions. The last player standing on each world won a portion of the prize pool. The winners on each finale world were ''Liquid statX'', ''SoloMission'', ''R3DL0RRYY'', ''Rot Rupper'', and ''CD Slay3r''.

==== Armageddon ====
{{main|Deadman: Armageddon}}
[[Deadman: Armageddon]] ran from [[19 July]] [[2024]] to [[3 August]] [[2024]]. The mechanics were similar to Deadman: Apocalypse, with an additional randomly-timed breach each day.

The finale was held on [[3 August]] [[2024]] on five finale worlds for different combat level brackets. Extensive allegations of rule-breaking during the event and finale led to the banning of several hundred accounts, including some who had originally been the last accounts standing. After investigations into this was complete, the winners were determined to be ''Taatuh DMM'', ''Antibone'', ''solo timbo'', and ''KalculatorV4'', with the 111 – 126 Bracket winner not announced.

==== Annihilation ====
{{main|Deadman: Annihilation}}
[[Deadman: Annihilation]] ran from [[30 January]] [[2026]] to [[20 February]] [[2026]]. The mechanics remained similar to the past two Deadman Mode variants, but with some key changes including the removal of [[bank key]]s and a reworked sigil system.

The finale was held on [[21 February]] [[2026]] on four finale worlds for different combat level brackets. The winners were determined to be ''Desailly'', ''KalculatorV4'', ''mama mia 73'', and ''Kalmaars''. A second finale was run on [[28 February]] [[2026]] for the 96+ combat bracket due to the first finale being unable to accommodate all players, the winner of which being ''HershyPurse6''.

==== Factions ====
{{main|Deadman: Factions}}
A short, 4-hour long event held on [[25 January]] [[2026]] with massively increased XP multipliers and drop rates, in which 4 factions of over 100 content creators battled to be the last standing. The winning team was Team Saradomin.

==== Mansions ====
{{main|Deadman Mansions}}
A real life event consisting of 10 content creators that included challenges both in and out of the game. It took place during the Autumn 2017 Deadman seasonals with the finale taking place live on stage at [[RuneFest]] 2017, and the winner was TeamLUL.

==Mechanics==
===Level brackets===
Players are divided into brackets based on their [[Combat level|combat levels]] to limit how large the power difference can be between the players one can attack or be attacked by while still using the same world. These level thresholds are '''3–75''', '''76–85''' and '''86–126'''.

Each bracket enjoys a 2x unique drop rate modifier.{{CiteGeneral|url=https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/permanent-deadman-world-345-improvements?oldschool=1|title=Permanent Deadman: World 345 Improvements|author=Jagex|date=12 March 2025|accessdate=2025-10-23}}

===Risk===
On permanent Deadman Mode servers, risking certain amounts of wealth and having no protection remaining is required to do some activities. To attack other players, you must be risking at least 100,000 GP in alch value, and if near a [[Deadman's chest (scenery)|Deadman's chest]], you must be risking at least 500,000 GP in alch value. To open a Deadman's chest, you must be risking at least 250,000 GP in alch value. To enter [[raid]] areas, you must be risking at least 1,000,000 GP in alch value.

If the player attempts to enter the raid area with protection and/or without enough risk, they will be force teleported out and take 35 [[hitpoints]] of damage, along with the message {{mes|You can't have protection remaining and must be risking at least 1,000,000 in alchemy value to enter this area.}} Going up the staircase leading towards the [[Chambers of Xeric]] without the required risk will result in being teleported to the area with [[rat]]s and dead soldiers and lizardmen north of [[Lacerta Falls]], entering [[Ver Sinhaza]] without the required risk will result in being teleported all the way north of [[Crombwick Manor]], and entering the [[Jaltevas Pyramid]] will result in being teleported to the area with a bunch of [[locust]]s near the [[Ruins of Ullek]]. However, entering the pyramid without risk in the first place can only be done when entering for the first time during [[Into the Tombs]]. Attempting to re-enter after the quest cutscene ends and you are teleported out will result in the message {{mes|You can't have protection remaining and must be risking at least 1,000,000 in alchemy value to enter.}}

If you enter the raids area with enough risk and then bank it, you will be teleported out after some time. The 35 damage dealt '''can''' kill you.

===Experience===
On permanent Deadman Mode servers, players will instead receive a scaling experience boost of 20x at level 1, to 10x at level 50 and beyond during their first 12 hour protection. With no protection, the boost is reduced to 10x at level 1, scaled to 5x at level 50 and beyond.

Experience points gained from quest rewards, XP lamps, and other rewards are multiplied similarly based on protection status and skill level. Players can disable or re-enable experience gain for combat skills or for [[Hitpoints]] by speaking with [[Nigel]] at [[Lumbridge Graveyard|Lumbridge graveyard]] or at any [[Deadman chest]].

Players will only have a 2x experience multiplier in most instanced areas. This includes instanced boss fights and the [[TzHaar Fight Cave]]. Exceptions include [[player-owned house]]s (Construction and Prayer experience only) and [[Tempoross]].

For seasonal Deadman Mode versions, see their corresponding main articles under [[Deadman_Mode#Variants|Deadman Mode variants]].

===Town protection===
[[File:Deadman Mode map.png|thumb|right|A map showing the safe (green) and [[multicombat zone|multi zones]] (red).<br>This map applies {{color|red|ONLY}} to W345. For the safezones used in Annihilation, see [[Deadman: Annihilation#Safezones|here]].]]

Some cities, towns and banks in the gamemode are guarded and are completely safe for unskulled players. Players inside these guarded areas can not be attacked by other players in it, nor can they be attacked from outside of it.

Any player who enters these guarded zones when [[skull (status)|skulled]] will be attacked on sight by level 1337 [[Guard (Deadman Mode)|guards]] and frozen for a short period of time by [[Wizguard|Wizguards]]. The melee and ranged guards have a fast attack speed and their damage ramps up the more consecutive hits you take, starting at 20% of the player's [[Hitpoints|hitpoints]] level.

===Skull status===
Attacking players first will always give you a 5-minute skull, even if you are attacking another skulled player. Attacking players during this skull timer always resets it back to 5 minutes and the timer will not deplete while in an instanced area or when standing on the same tile for over around a minute. Remaining time will be shown on the game HUD and it refreshes every half a minute.

In Deadman Mode, being [[skull (status)|skulled]] will prevent you from accessing the guarded areas, as level 1337 [[Guard (Deadman Mode)|guards]] and [[Wizguard|Wizguards]] will immediately attack the player on entry. This means that the skulled player has to utilise banks that are not in safe-zones and these become hotspots for player killers.
[[File:Deadman Mode Timer Chat Interface.png|thumb|A 7 second timer interface that shows up when players attempt to logout.]]
Regardless of skull status, attempting to [[Logout button|log out]], enter a [[Portal (Player-owned house)|portal (Player-owned house)]], or use most forms of non-teleport transportation such as [[Lovakengj Minecart Network|minecarts]] will  bring up a 7 second timer interface that will begin counting down in the chatbox. While this interface is active, the player must not perform any further actions or be attacked. After the timer interface stays open without interruption for 7 seconds, the player will automatically perform the requested action.

Skulled players will not be able to teleport instantly even if they are out of combat, as they will receive the 7 second timer interface when they attempt to teleport. If a player manages to kill another player in a [[single-combat zone]], they are granted a 1-minute grace period when they cannot be attacked. Attacking, dying or logging out will cause you to lose your remaining grace period.

Unskulled players will be able to teleport instantly as long as they have not been attacked by a player or NPC in the last 7 seconds. If a unskulled player has been attacked recently and attempts to teleport, they will instead receive the following game message: {{mes|You must be out of combat for another X seconds to teleport.}} In this case, players can perform further actions without restarting the countdown because they do not receive the 7 second timer interface that they must keep open.

===Death===
The consequences of dying in Deadman Mode largely resemble those of dying in [[High risk world|high risk]] worlds, meaning no items on the player are protected on PvP death, and the [[Protect Item]] prayer cannot be used.

Most items on the player are not kept in inventory on death:
*Upon PvM death outside of instances, players keep 3 of their most valuable protected items if unskulled (none if skulled). All unprotected tradeable items drop to the floor and '''become visible to everyone after 1 minute'''. All unprotected untradeable items go to a [[gravestone]], where they can be collected.
*Upon PvP death, all tradeable items and untradeable items that can revert to a tradeable form drop to the floor for the killer. Other untradeable items go to a [[gravestone]], where they can be collected.

Additionally, players can change their [[respawn point]] to the [[Grand Exchange]] by speaking to [[Brugsen Bursen]].

===Breaches===
Boss breaches familiar appear on the permanent Deadman world every weekend in four-hour intervals, starting on Saturday at 02:00 GMT and ending on Sunday at 22:00 GMT. There are two types of breaches: 
* Localised breach in two locations, of which one is in [[single-way combat]] and other in [[multicombat]].
* A region breach in which the breach monsters spawn widely across given region.
Monsters spawn from a [[Boss Spawn]] created by the projectiles that flow out of the [[Breach (scenery)|Breach]] spawners. In localised breaches, the monsters can spawn maximum of 9 tiles away in any direction from the spawner. The breach will keep spawning bosses for around 15 minutes, and the ones left unkilled will despawn around 30 minutes later. Efficacy of [[ruby bolts (e)]] special attacks is limited on breach monsters, having a max hit of 30.

Players with PvP protection active cannot attack breach monsters at either breach location on the permanent Deadman world.

===Deadman's chest===
Once every hour, a [[Deadman's chest (scenery)|Deadman's chest]] will spawn alongside [[Zemouregal (Deadman)#Deadman's chest|Zemouregal]], who can summon [[Zemouregal Summon]]s. The chest will spawn in a random location, with its location broadcast in the chatbox to all players on the world: {{mes|A Deadman's Chest has spawned at [location]!|colour=#a53fff}}

The chest contains a variety of powerful items, but takes 60 seconds without interruption to open (30 seconds in a multi-combat area). Any form of combat or inventory action (e.g. eating food/drinking a potion) will reset the opening timer. Additionally, the following conditions must be met in order to attempt opening it:
* The player is not in an instance
* The player is risking at least 250,000 coins in alchable equivalent value
* The player does not have immunity or PvP protection

The first player to successfully open the chest will receive one of the following:
* [[Ancient Warriors' equipment|Ancient Warriors' armours]]
* [[The dogsword (Deadman Mode)|The dogsword]]
* [[Archaic_emblem#Tier_5|Tier 5 Archaic emblem]]

===Charms===
Charms give various boosts in [[PvM]], skilling and gathering while they are in the player's inventory or inside a [[looting bag]] in the player's inventory. There is no restriction on the amount of charms in use simultaneously. They are dropped by breach bosses and NPCs of over 200 combat level. The charms include:
*[[Accumulation charm]], boosts clue scrolls rates, hunting chinchompas, gathering and runecraft. It can also be toggled to receive planks instead of logs when woodcutting.
*[[Nimbleness charm]], provides unlimited run energy, prevents natural prayer drain, boosts agility (including gathering marks of grace), and boosts pickpocketing.
*[[Stockpiling charm]], boosts various minigame rewards, stackable clues, and notes gathering resources.
*[[Vulnerability charm]], boosts PvM in terms of accuracy, reduced damage and improved thralls. Also heals for 10% of damage dealt.

====Rate of access====
[[File:Deadman global table denominator.png|thumb|The denominator of the global loot table, with regular monsters in blue and boss monsters in red]]
The rate of accessing this table depends on the combat level of the monster.{{CiteTwitter|author=Mod Ash|url=https://twitter.com/JagexAsh/status/1580838612991504384|date=14 October 2022|archiveurl=https://reldo.runescape.wiki/i/103508|archivedate=19 February 2023}}

The probability of rolling for this table after killing a monster with combat level <math>L</math> and monster type modifier <math>M</math> is:

For monsters level 1 to 100:
:<math>\mathbb{P} = \frac{1}{(1000 + \left\lfloor (100 - L)^2 \right\rfloor) * M}</math>
For monsters level 101 to 500:
:<math>\mathbb{P} = \frac{1}{(\left\lfloor 1187.5 - L * 1.875 \right\rfloor) * M}</math>.
For monsters level 501 or above:
:<math>\mathbb{P} = \frac{1}{250 * M}</math>

The monster type modifier <math>M</math> is equal to 16 if a regular monster is killed, while <math>M</math> equals 4 if the killed NPC is a boss monster. Any of the Wilderness monsters that can be assigned as a [[Boss#Boss slayer|boss Slayer task]] counts as a boss monster for the purpose of the boosted global drop table on the permanent Deadman server.{{CiteTwitter|author=Mod Ash|url=https://twitter.com/JagexAsh/status/1597267639038251009|date=28 November 2022|archiveurl=https://reldo.runescape.wiki/i/107538|archivedate=19 February 2023}}

==Historical mechanics==
===Bank Raiding===
Prior to [[Update:Wyrmscraig & Sailing Changes| an update]] on [[5 August]] [[2026]], bank raiding on PvP deaths was a core mechanic of Deadman Mode.

Players who were killed by another player would also drop a [[bank key]], in which half of 10 most valuable items stacks, based on [[Grand Exchange]] value, were removed from their bank. All Grand Exchange slots would also be permanently wiped, preventing players from trying to safely store items there.

The player that obtained the key could collect the items by opening a [[Deadman chest|chest]] in a bank within a safe-zone, which meant they had to wait until their skull expired. These bank keys could not be deposited into a bank, and only five bank keys could be in a player's possession at any given time. 

Players could insure up to ten tradeable items (ten single items, not 10 stacks of items) by speaking to the [[Financial Wizard]] and depositing the items in the safe deposit box. Items in the safe deposit box would not be lost, unlike those found in the bank, and could be collected any time the player visited the Financial Wizard at almost any bank in a safe-zone.

[[Deadman chest]]s and [[Financial Wizard]]s can still be found in Deadman Mode even though bank keys have been removed.

===Experience Loss===
Prior to [[Update:World 45 & PvP Changes|an update]] on [[4 October]] [[2023]], losing experience on PvP deaths was a core mechanic of Deadman Mode.

Historically, players who died to players would lose experience in accordance to the combat level differences. The exact formula was:

<math>\frac{1}{1 + e^y}</math>

Where:

<math>y=\frac{CombatLevel (killer)-\frac{121}{125}*CombatLevel (victim)-30}{10}</math>

Players could choose to preserve a maximum of five skills (two combat skills and three non-combat skills) to prevent experience loss. However, a player's [[Herblore]] level would never be less than 3, provided they had completed [[Druidic Ritual]], and a player's [[Hitpoints]] level will never be less than 10. In addition, players could purchase a life insurance to prevent their Hitpoints level from falling below certain thresholds. After its removal, refunds for bought insurances could be claimed from [[Gelin]].

==Trivia==
*There was a [[World 347 glitch|glitch]] on [[24 April]] [[2018]] where the permanent Deadman Mode world was temporarily swapped for scheduled maintenance, however Deadman Mode mechanics were prematurely applied on the new Deadman Mode world and players were subject to Deadman Mode conditions that affected their main server accounts. Jagex attempted to restore items and experience lost by the glitch.
