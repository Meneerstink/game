'''Annihilation blueprints''' can be used to change a [[player-owned house]]'s appearance to the [[Wilderness (theme)|Wilderness theme]] [[house style]]. Having the [[Estate agent]] re-decorate your house into the Wilderness house style while having the blueprints in the inventory will consume them and permanently unlock the house style.

Upon unlocking the house style, a set of [[Annihilation exit portal blueprints]] will replace the consumed Annihilation blueprints in the player's inventory, which can then be used to recolour the exit portal. These exit portal blueprints can be infinitely reclaimed from [[Nigel]] in [[Lumbridge]].

==Setting up an orange exit portal==
Due to how [[exit portal]]s work in player-owned houses, additional steps are required to replace the normal purple exit portal with the orange Wilderness-themed one. 

* Build a new [[Garden]] room in your POH. This will not be permanent.
* In the new garden, build a basic [[exit portal]].
* Remove the original exit portal and build an Annihilation exit portal (using the [[Annihilation exit portal blueprints]]) in its place.
* Remove the second garden, if you wish.
