The '''volatile Nightmare staff''' is a one-handed [[Staves|staff]] that is created by attaching a [[volatile orb]] to the [[Nightmare staff]] granting it a high damaging [[Volatile Nightmare staff#Special attack|special attack]]. Requiring level 82 [[Magic]] and 50 [[Hitpoints]] to equip, it can autocast either offensive [[standard spells]] or [[Ancient Magicks]]. Like the [[Nightmare staff]] it gives a 15% bonus in [[magic damage]]. The upgraded staff is untradeable, but it can be reverted to its tradeable components at any time. If lost on death in the [[Wilderness]], the killer will receive the staff and the orb.

==Special attack==
[[File:Immolate.gif|thumb|250px|A player performing the volatile Nightmare staff's special attack, ''Immolate''.]]
The volatile Nightmare staff has a [[special attack]], ''Immolate'', that consumes 55% of the player's special attack energy to hit the target with 50% increased accuracy and deals a high amount of damage. The special attack does not consume runes.

When calculating the max hit, Immolate acts like any other spell. The spell's max hit is calculated first and then rounded down. All [[magic damage]] gear, including the +15% from the staff is calculated separately. Because the staff has a magic damage bonus, the max hit seen in-game will always be higher than the spell's max hit. The max hit varies with visible Magic level according to the formula below.{{CiteTwitter|author=Mod Ash|url=https://twitter.com/JagexAsh/status/1765337358294003882|date=06 March 2024|archiveurl=https://reldo.runescape.wiki/i/108809|archivedate=03 May 2026|quote=[What is the formula for the Volatile staff's special attack base maxhit?] The base is 58, scaled up or down by your *visible* Magic stat divided by 99, if I'm reading my colleagues' code correctly.}} The spell's max hit is limited to, at most, 58. This means that even though the max hit is based on the player's magic level, [[Temporary skill boost|boosting]] above Magic level 98 will not increase the max hit. It can be increased though by using additional magic damage gear including the [[slayer helmet (i)]] when on a [[slayer task]].

<math>\begin{align}
\text{Spell Max Hit} &= \min\left\{ \left\lfloor\ 58 * \frac{\text{Magic Level}}{99} + 1 \right\rfloor\ , 58 \right\} \\
\text{Staff Max Hit} &= \left\lfloor\ \text{Spell Max Hit} * 1.15 \right\rfloor\ \\
\end{align}</math>
