The '''trinket of vengeance''' is an item in [[Deadman Mode]] and its seasonal variants [[Deadman: Apocalypse|Apocalypse]] and [[Deadman: Armageddon|Armageddon]] which casts the [[Vengeance]] spell. Though it has no requirements to use, it retains the 30 second casting cooldown that the spell has.

Like the [[Deadman: Apocalypse/Sigils|sigils]], [[Deadman: Apocalypse#Trinkets|trinkets]] are dropped by most monsters (via the [[Deadman:_Apocalypse/Global_loot_table|Deadman Mode drop table]]) and are tradeable on the [[Grand Exchange]].

This trinket is not accessible in [[Deadman: Annihilation]]. In [[Deadman: All Stars Season 3]], a version of the trinket that had only two charges before being destroyed was available.
