The '''Armageddon weapon scroll''' is an item that can be purchased from the [[Deadman Reward Store]] for 12,000 [[Deadman: Apocalypse/Points|Deadman Points]], which is earned from participating in [[Deadman: Armageddon]].

It can be brought to [[Nigel]] in order to change the [[Armadyl godsword]], [[dark bow]], [[voidwaker]], and [[volatile Nightmare staff]] to resemble their corrupt variants from Deadman: Armageddon. When you give the scroll to [[Nigel]], it will permanently unlock all weapon cosmetics for the player and remove the scroll permanently. You can then freely switch any of the weapons<ref>OldSchoolRS; Let’s Talk Deadman: Armageddon! stream. https://www.youtube.com/live/enmaX1RmXLw?t=1656s</ref> between their standard and deadman variant at [[Nigel]], and it can be applied to however many of the item you wish.

Upon PvP death the PK'er will receive the normal variant of the item.<ref>OldSchoolRS; Let's Talk Deadman: Armageddon! stream. https://www.youtube.com/live/enmaX1RmXLw?t=1670s</ref>
