The '''Armageddon teleport scroll''' is an item that can be purchased from the [[Deadman Reward Store]] for 9,000 [[Deadman: Apocalypse/Points|Deadman Points]], which is earned from participating in [[Deadman: Armageddon]]. It can be used to unlock a Deadman: Armageddon-themed [[Home Teleport]] [[animation override]]. To enable it, players can right click "Animation" on the Home Teleport spell icon in their spellbook and select "Armageddon."
