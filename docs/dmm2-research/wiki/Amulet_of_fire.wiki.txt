The '''amulet of fire''' is an amulet requiring level 59 [[Magic]] to equip. It is made by using a [[fire ruby]] on the [[Fire Altar]], along with an [[amulet of magic]] and 100 [[fire rune]]s; creating the amulet requires level 21 [[Runecraft]], granting 125 experience. It can also be created when accessing the altars via [[Guardians of the Rift]], as they are the same altars as outside the minigame.

Alongside identical stats to an amulet of magic, the amulet of fire adds a +2 max hit to [[fire spells]], which is added before [[magic damage]] and [[elemental weakness]] modifiers are applied, and therefore scales with both. Additionally, this +2 max hit stacks additively with the bonus from the [[chaos gauntlets]], for a total of +5 max hits when casting [[bolt spells]]. Lastly, the max hit bonus also works against [[Salarin the twisted]].

The amulet of fire, alongside the [[Amulet of air|amulets of air]], [[Amulet of water|water]], and [[Amulet of earth|earth]], can be combined into an [[elemental amulet]] at any elemental [[runic altar]], which combines the effects of all four amulets into one. Doing so requires level 30 [[Runecraft]] and 500 of each elemental rune, granting 175 experience.
