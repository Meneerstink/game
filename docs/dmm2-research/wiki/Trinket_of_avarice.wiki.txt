The '''trinket of avarice''' is an item in [[Deadman Mode]]. It allows loot to be automatically noted and picked up. It will also put loot into [[looting bag]]s if possible. The value option can be used to adjust what items are automatically picked up, based on their value.

It can be acquired as a drop from monsters found during [[breach]]es.
