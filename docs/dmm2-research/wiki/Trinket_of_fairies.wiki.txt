The '''trinket of fairies''' is an item in [[Deadman Mode]] that allows players to remotely access [[fairy rings]], [[spirit tree]]s, and [[tool leprechaun]]s that they have access to. The trinket cannot be used beyond level 30 [[Wilderness]].

It can be acquired as a drop from monsters found during [[breach]]es.
