The '''Annihilation weapon scroll''' can be used to cosmetically change the [[toxic staff of the dead]], [[Bow of Faerdhinen (c)]], or all three pieces of [[Crystal equipment#Crystal armour|crystal armour]]. It can be purchased from the [[Deadman Reward Store]] for 12,000 [[Deadman: Apocalypse/Points|Deadman Points]], which are earned from participating in seasonal [[Deadman]] events.

The scroll can be used by bringing it to [[Nigel]]. When you give the scroll to [[Nigel]], it will permanently unlock the 5 cosmetics for the player and remove the scroll permanently. You can then freely switch any of the items{{CiteReddit|author=Mod Goblin|url=https://np.reddit.com/r/2007scape/comments/1qkr9lh/deadman_annihilation_everything_you_need_to_know/o18lh98/|title=Deadman: Annihilation - Everything You Need to Know|quote=12k points, permanent unlock that you can use on all affected items, so one scroll can do all armour pieces + bow!|date=23 January 2026|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/apzmT|archivedate=2026-01-31}} between their standard and deadman variant at [[Nigel]], and it can be applied to however many of the item you wish.

Crystal equipment reverted from the Deadman variant will be reset to the default colours of the item; the [[Crystal equipment#Recolouring|Meilyr clan colours]] for the armour, and the [[Bow of Faerdhinen (c)|Hefin clan colours]] for the bow.

Upon PvP death the PK'er will receive the normal variant of the item.<ref>OldSchoolRS; Let's Talk Deadman: Armageddon! stream. https://www.youtube.com/live/enmaX1RmXLw?t=1670s</ref>
