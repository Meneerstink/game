'''Deadman starter packs''' are crates obtained from [[Brugsen Bursen]] and the [[Melee combat tutor|melee]], [[magic combat tutor|magic]], or [[ranged combat tutor]]s in seasonal [[Deadman Mode]]. They are obtained on death if not toggled off, and cannot be brought to [[Entrana]]. 11 free inventory spaces are required to open it.

The pack contains the following items:
*[[Starter sword]]
*[[Starter bow]]
*[[Starter staff]]
*12 [[Tuna (Deadman starter pack)|tunas]]
*[[Chronicle]] with 10 charges added to it
*[[Deadman's chest]]
*[[Deadman's legs]]
*[[Starter cape]]
*[[Dramen staff]]
*[[Deadman's skull]]
