The '''corrupted twisted bow''' is a ranged weapon that requires level 70 [[Ranged]] to wield. Exclusive to [[Deadman Mode]] seasonals, it can be acquired by opening a [[trinket of advanced weaponry]] obtained from a [[Deadman:_Apocalypse#Breaches|breach]].

As a corrupted variant of the [[twisted bow]], it has 20% lower bonuses than the regular variant, with its [[Ranged Strength]] reduced from +20 to +2.

Like the normal variant, the [[Budget Setup]] combat achievement will not be completed if the bow is brought into [[The Inferno]].
