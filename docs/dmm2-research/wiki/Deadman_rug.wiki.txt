A '''deadman rug''' can be built in the [[rug space]] of the [[parlour]], [[bedroom]], [[skill hall]], [[quest hall]], [[chapel]], [[Dungeon (Construction)|dungeon]], and [[portal nexus]] in a [[player-owned house]]. It requires 28 [[Construction]] to build and gives 283 [[experience]] when built.

If the furniture is removed, players will receive their [[Armageddon rug]] back, allowing them to sell it if they so wish. Note that players '''will not''' get their rug back if the room the rug is built in is deleted.
