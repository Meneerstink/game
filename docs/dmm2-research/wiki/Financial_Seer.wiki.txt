'''Financial Seers''' act as [[Financial Wizard]]s in [[Neitiznot]], only appearing in [[Deadman Mode]] and located in the bank. Players can give them ten items that they wish to keep safe; when the 10 items are given to the Financial Seer, they are securely stored in the Safe Deposit Box and cannot be used.
[[File:Deadman_mode_-_Safe_Deposit_Box.png|thumb|left|The Safe Deposit Box, accessed by visiting the Financial Seer.]]
Players cannot store untradeable items. Stackable items such as bank notes, runes, arrows, bolts, etc. will not count as one item. For example, 10 [[air rune]]s will count as 10 items.
