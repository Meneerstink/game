'''Deadman: All Stars Season 3''' was the third season of [[Deadman: All Stars]], an invite-only variant of [[Deadman Mode]] running from [[6 June]] [[2026]] to [[15 June]] [[2026]]. As with seasons 1 and 2, it was hosted and spearheaded by content creator Solo Mission, in collaboration with Jagex.

The finale took place on [[20 June]] [[2026]], and was held at a live in-person event at the Rosemont Theatre in Rosemont, Illinois, USA.

==Teams==
Each of the 6 teams was led by a team captain, who drafted their team members using a snake draft during a livestream at 19<span title="19:00 UTC">:00 UTC</span> on [[31 May]] [[2026]]. '''Bold contestants''' were new for season 3.

==Format==
Starting with fresh accounts, each team had 9 days to build accounts on a private [[Deadman]] world, from [[6 June]] [[2026]] to [[15 June]] [[2026]]. Competitors were limited to 120 total hours playtime each, with 6 hours of enforced downtime per day, with the server being closed from <span title="04:00 UTC">05:00 BST (0:00 EDT)</span> to <span title="10:00 UTC">11:00</span> BST (6:00 EDT) each day. There was also a midweek break, with the world being closed on June 11. The world opened June 6th at <span title="12:00 UTC">13:00</span> BST (8:00 EDT).

The event was streamed by most of the competitors, as well as an official commentary stream hosted by Solo Mission and PureSpam.

===Finale event===
The in-person finale event occurred at 13:00 CDT (19:00 BST) [[20 June]] [[2026]] at the Rosemont Theatre in Rosemont, Illinois, USA.

Pre-sale tickets were made available to active players using the [[Jagex Launcher]] on [[27 February]] [[2026]] 17:00 BST through link displayed on a banner. General sale tickets were made available 24 hours after the pre-sale via Ticketmaster.

Three ticket tiers were available: a standard ticket, the Rune Package ticket, and the Dragon Package ticket.

Standard tickets include:
* Seat in the audience
* Full access to event
* Free gifts on entry

Rune Package tickets include:
* Some of the best seating in the house
* Goodie Bag
* 15% Discount on all in-event merchandise
* Early entry into the venue.

Dragon Package tickets include:
* The best seating in the house
* Goodie Bag
* 20% Discount on all in-event merchandise
* Early entry into venue
* Open bar and open catering for the entire event in private VIP lounge
* Commemorative event lanyard

Event-exclusive merchandise was also available for sale during the event, and was made available to preorder via the Ticketmaster link as an event extra.

The event was streamed on the ''Old School RuneScape'' Twitch channel, hosted by Purespam and Mod Manked, to provide an easy way to follow the action and offer commentary on teams' strategies and performance.

==Mechanics==
Most game mechanics worked similarly to the seasonal [[Deadman Mode]] events. Drop rates were boosted 6x and XP rates were boosted 15x for all skills outside of [[instance]]s, 3x for combat skills in instances, and no boost for non-combat skills in instances.

===Reverted [[Deadman: Annihilation]] changes===
Various changes from Deadman: Annihilation, intended to make the game mode more accessible to casual players, were reverted.{{CiteVideo|author=Solo Mission|title=DEADMAN ALLSTARS SEASON 3 ft Odablock, B0aty, Framed, and more....|url=4nzHRYEe4io|timestamp=4:22|date=|site=|notes=|name=}}

* [[Bank key]]s were reinstated.
* [[Deadman's chest (scenery)|Deadman's chests]] were removed.
* [[Ancient Warriors' equipment|Ancient Warriors' armour]] was removed.
** Some exceptions such as breaches, missions, and blood money. See below for more details.

All [[safezone]]s except for [[Varrock]] were removed. Players spawned, respawned, and could [[home teleport]] to the [[Grand Exchange]]. [[Safe deposit box]]es were removed and replaced with a designated NPC for each team which could hold up to 40 tradeable items, accessible by any member of a team.

These designated NPCs were:

===Breaches===
Breaches made a return, functioning similarly to those in [[Deadman: All Stars Season 2|season 2]]. They spawned once a day at <span title="19:00 UTC">20:00 BST (15:00 EDT)</span> in a multi-combat area and lasted for 30 minutes. [[Breach monster]]s drop [[Ancient Warriors' equipment|Ancient Warriors' weapons]] at a rate in 1/90 and [[corrupted weapons]] at a rate of 1/80, and attacking a breach monster [[Skull (status)|skulls]] the player. The breaches took place in the following locations:
* Day 1: [[Mole Hole]]
* Day 2: [[Rogues' Castle]]
* Day 3: [[Al Kharid]] 
** Multicombat area expanded to the south and east to include the [[Shantay Pass]] and [[Kalphite Cave]] entrance
* Day 4: Middle of the [[Wilderness]]
* Day 5: South [[Varlamore]] (near the [[Colossal Wyrm Remains]])
* Day 6: [[Mor Ul Rek]]
* Day 7: [[Ape Atoll]]
* Day 8: [[Port Piscarilius]] 
** Multicombat area expanded to the north-east to include the bank and [[anglerfish]] fishing area

===Missions===
Twice a day, a mission was posted for all teams, at <span title="15:00 UTC">16:00 BST (11:00 EDT)</span> and <span title="23:00 UTC">00:00 BST (19:00 EDT)</span>. Missions varied in difficulty but always required a specific boss or monster to be killed. The monster dropped a [[mission totem]], which must be brought back to the totem trader<!--not an NPC, but rather an account operated by the casters--> at the [[Grand Exchange]] to complete the mission and claim the reward. The player currently holding the totem was marked with a red [[Skull (status)|skull]], meaning they couldn't safely enter the safezone around [[Varrock]], and if killed in PvP, the totem was lost to the player who killed them, who could turn the totem in for their team instead.

Rewards for turning in a mission totem varied from tier 4 (gear useful for progression) to tier 1 (best-in-slot gear for the finale), with later missions rewarding better gear.{{CiteVideo|author=Solo Mission|title=DEADMAN ALLSTARS SEASON 3 ft Odablock, B0aty, Framed, and more....|url=4nzHRYEe4io|timestamp=7:32|date=|site=|notes=|name=}}

* Tier 1 example power level: [[Vesta's longsword (Deadman Mode)|Vesta's longsword]], [[Zuriel's staff (Deadman Mode)|Zuriel's staff]]
* Tier 2 example power level: [[Armadyl chestplate]], [[Virtus robe bottom]]
* Tier 3 example power level: [[Corrupted weapons]], [[Morrigan's throwing axe (Deadman Mode)|Morrigan's throwing axes]]
* Tier 4 example power level: [[Statius's warhammer (Deadman Mode)|Statius's warhammer]], [[Vesta's spear (Deadman Mode)|Vesta's spear]]

While teams did not know the exact mission or reward in advance, they did know the tier that each mission awards.

When the mission totem is dropped after defeating the target, a global message of {{mes|[Target] has been defeated.}} was sent.

===Blood money===
Killing other players during the week rewarded the killer's team with [[blood money]]. Kills in [[single-way combat]] or later in the week will reward more blood money than those in [[multicombat area]]s, at breaches, or earlier in the week. Dying removed 1 blood money from a team's total, regardless of time or location. A team could never have a negative amount of blood money.

===Finale rules===
Sigils were banned during the finale. Other banned items, spellbooks, actions, etc. include:
* Entity Hider, ::renderself, or the Death Dotter plug-in.
* The [[Lunar spellbook|Lunar]] or [[Arceuus spellbook]]s
* [[Phoenix necklace]]s
* [[Prayer regeneration potion]]s
* Extra [[Hitpoints]] gain through non-PvP means
** Hitpoints gained from [[special attacks]] and [[blood spells]] is allowed
* [[Rock cake]]s, [[locator orb]]s, or any other item that lowers a player's Hitpoints intentionally
* More than 1 [[ring of recoil]]
** [[Ring of suffering|Rings of suffering]] can have any number of charges
* Drinking [[potions]] before a player enters for their team's match
** Players can pre-pot "in line" while waiting for their turn once their team's match has begun
* Pre-seeding the arena with [[Mithril seeds|mithril]] or [[adamant seeds]]
* A player placing bets on themselves or other teams

All players began with a base inventory consisting of a [[ranging potion]], [[supercombat potion]], three [[Sanfew serum]]s, two [[Saradomin brew]]s, and nine [[anglerfish]] or [[manta ray]]s. One food for each player could be replaced by a Saradomin brew by spending [[#Blood money|blood money]] earned by killing other players throughout the week.

===Finale===
The finale has two stages, a group stage and a bracket. Teams are seeded based on blood money earned. The fights all took place in an altered version of the [[1v1_Arena#All_Stars_Season_3|1v1 Arena]].

During the group stage, the teams are divided into two groups of three each. Each team will fight one match each against the other teams in their group, with the top teams from each group advancing to the semifinals and the semifinal winners advancing to the finals.

Each match is a continuous series of 1v1 fights, starting with the last-row pick for each team and the next teammate stepping in when their teammate dies. The winner is the last team with members still alive.
