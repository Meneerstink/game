The '''trinket of undead''' is an item in [[Deadman Mode]] that allows players to summon a special [[thrall]] of the player's choice, being either [[TzRek-Zuk]], the [[Kalphite Princess#Flying|Kalphite Princess]], or [[Wisp]]. These thralls function the same as greater thralls, functioning as a melee, ranged, or magic thrall respectively. Though the trinket has no requirements to use, it does have a 10 second cooldown.

Being identical to regular greater thralls, the trinket's thralls always deal [[successful hit]]s, with the damage roll ranging from 0-3. The damage roll can be increased to 0-15 with the [[sigil of the augmented thrall]].

It can be acquired as a drop from monsters found during [[breach]]es, as well as from looting the [[Deadman's chest (scenery)|Deadman's chest]].
