The '''corrupted voidwaker''' is a melee weapon that requires level 65 [[Attack]] to wield. Exclusive to [[Deadman Mode]] seasonals, it can be acquired by opening a [[trinket of advanced weaponry]] obtained from a [[Deadman:_Apocalypse#Breaches|breach]].

As a corrupted variant of the [[voidwaker]], it has 20% lower bonuses than the regular variant.
