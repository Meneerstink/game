The '''Armageddon cape fabric''' is an item that can be purchased from the [[Deadman Reward Store]] for 6,000 [[Deadman: Apocalypse/Points|Deadman Points]], which is earned from participating in [[Deadman: Armageddon]].

It can be brought to [[Nigel]] in order to change the [[imbued god capes]] to a Deadman-themed cosmetic variant. When you give the fabric to [[Nigel]], it will permanently unlock all cape cosmetics for the player. This process is not reversible. You can then freely switch any of the capes{{CiteVideo|author=Old School RuneScape|title=OldSchool RS;  Let’s Talk Deadman: Armageddon! <nowiki>|</nowiki> OSRS Q&A Livestream July 18th |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enmaX1RmXLw|timestamp=35:42 - 36:05|date=18 July 2024|site=YouTube|notes=|name=}} between their standard and deadman variant at [[Nigel]], and it can be applied to however many of the item you wish.

Upon PvP death, the cape will become [[broken]], the cosmetic is removed, and the killer receives 96,000 coins. After repairing, the player can go back to Nigel to reapply the cosmetic.

==Products==
To make the [[Imbued ancient cape (Deadman)]], you must use the [[ancient fabric]] on one of the other Deadman imbued god capes.
