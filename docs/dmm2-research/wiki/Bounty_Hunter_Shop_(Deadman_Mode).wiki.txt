The '''Bounty Hunter Shop''' is a store run by the [[Emblem Trader#Grand Exchange|Emblem Trader]] at the [[Grand Exchange]], which is accessible on both permanent and seasonal [[Deadman Mode]] worlds.

[[Archaic emblem]]s can be handed in at the Emblem Trader in exchange for store credit, which can spent on a wide variety of supplies and ornaments. Participating in Wilderness Slayer yields a chance of receiving a tier 1 emblem as an uncommon drop from the assigned monsters, while tier 5 emblems can be received from any NPC with a combat level of 100 or higher at a rate of 1/300.

During the [[Deadman: Annihilation]] game mode, tier 1 and tier 5 archaic emblems may be exchanged at the Emblem Trader for 100,000 and 1,500,000 archaic points respectively. On the permanent Deadman world, they are worth 50,000 and 750,000 points.
