'''The dogsword''' was a [[Godsword]] combining the effects of all 5 godswords. It was an unlock in the [[Grid Master]] [[community event]], unlocked by completing all tasks in column 1. As with all godsword variants, it requires level 75 [[Attack]] to wield.

It counts as a god item towards all factions in the [[God Wars Dungeon]].

==Special attack==
[[File:Echo slash.webm|thumb|440x440px|A player performing the dogsword's special attack, ''Echo slash''.]]The dogsword has a [[special attack]], ''Echo slash'', which consumes 50% of the wielder's special attack energy. The special attack applies all of the [[godsword]]s' special attacks at once. There's also a chance on rare occasions that when performing its special attack the player will shout {{Overhead|Woof!}} and a different special attack sound will play instead.

== Trivia ==
* The dogsword can be used as one of the three items for the [[Clue scroll (master)|master]] [[Treasure Trails/Guide/Cryptic clues/Master|cryptic clue]] step "''Here, there are tears, but nobody is crying. Speak to the guardian and show off your alignment to balance.''", requiring the player to talk to [[Juna]] while wearing three [[Guthix items|Guthix-affiliated items]]. Despite no direct reference to [[Guthix]] on the hilt, Juna's dialogue compliments the player's dedication to balance.
* Equipping the dogsword does not count towards the task to equip a godsword.
