The '''Deadman Reward Store''' is a store run by [[Nigel]] at the [[Grand Exchange]]. He sells and buys rewards from [[Deadman: Armageddon]] and [[Deadman: Annihilation]] in exchange for [[Deadman Points]]. The Deadman Points balance is separate for both main game and Deadman Mode, which means that they can be spent on both gamemodes without affecting the other. The points accumulate between events.

'''Note''': You need to log onto the permanent Deadman Mode world 345 after the event ends to have your Deadman Points be available in the main game.
