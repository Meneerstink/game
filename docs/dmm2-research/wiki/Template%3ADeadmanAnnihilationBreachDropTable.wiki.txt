<includeonly>==Drops==
{{DropLogProject|kills=1368089}}

Drops are received by the first 16 players to deal damage to a breach monster. There is a tertiary roll for rare drops, then two rolls for non-unique drops. After both regular drops are decided, there is a 50% chance that one of them is replaced by chitin.

===Rare drops===
