The '''corrupted dark bow''' is a ranged weapon that requires level 50 [[Ranged]] to wield. Exclusive to [[Deadman: Armageddon]], it can be acquired by opening a [[Trinket of advanced weaponry]] obtained from a [[Deadman:_Armageddon#Breaches|breach]].

As a corrupted variant of the [[dark bow]], it has an attack range of 6 tiles rather than 10. It also fires arrows 2 ticks faster than the regular dark bow.
