The '''Annihilation teleport scroll''' is an item that can be purchased from the [[Deadman Reward Store]] for 10,000 [[Deadman: Apocalypse/Points|Deadman Points]], which are earned from participating in seasonal [[Deadman]] events. It can be used to unlock a [[Lever (Deserted Keep)|Wilderness lever]] themed [[Home Teleport]] [[animation override]]. To enable it, players can right click "Animation" on the Home Teleport spell icon in their spellbook and select "Annihilation."
