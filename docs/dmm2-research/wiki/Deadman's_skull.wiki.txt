The '''Deadman's skull''' is an item in [[Deadman: Annihilation]] that players can interact with to purchase sigils and quest lamps from the [[Deadman: Annihilation/Skull shop|skull shop]]. Duplicate sigils can be sold back to the skull shop by using them on the deadman's skull while in your inventory. [[Archaic emblem]]s can also be exchanged for points by using them on the skull.

If lost, it can be reclaimed from the [[Emblem Trader]] at the [[Grand Exchange]].

==Features==
===Unlocks===
The "Unlocks" right-click option opens an interface showing all [[Deadman: Annihilation/Skull shop|sigils]] available, showing which have or have not been purchased. Toggleable sigils (such as the [[Sigil of Remote Storage]]) can be turned on and off by clicking them.

===Swap===
The "Swap" right-click option allows for swapping between different [[spellbook]]s and [[prayer]] books. A spellbook can only be swapped to if the prerequisites have been fulfilled (e.g. completion of [[Lunar Diplomacy]] to use the [[lunar spellbook]]). To swap to the [[Ruinous Powers]] prayer book, a player must buy the [[Ruinous powers (Deadman Mode)|Ruinous Powers tome]] from the skull shop.

Swapping prayers can be done anywhere and while in combat. However, swapping spellbooks can only be done within a [[safezone]] and out of combat.

===Breach check===
The "Breach Check" right-click option displays the time to the next [[breach]].
