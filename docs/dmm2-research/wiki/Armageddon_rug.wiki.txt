The '''Armageddon rug''' is an item that can be purchased from the [[Deadman Reward Store]] for 3,000 [[Deadman: Apocalypse/Points|Deadman Points]], which is earned from participating in [[Deadman: Armageddon]]. It is used to build a [[deadman rug]] within a [[player-owned house]].
