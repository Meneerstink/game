The Deadman '''imbued ancient cape''' is an ornamented variant of the [[imbued ancient cape]], made by giving [[Nigel]] an [[Armageddon cape fabric]] to unlock the god cape transformations, and using the [[ancient fabric]] on one of the imbued Deadman god capes. Unlike the others, it cannot be made by using an imbued ancient cape on Nigel. This cape, like its base component, counts as a [[Zaros#Affiliated_items|Zarosian item]] for [[God Wars Dungeon]].

If the player dies a PvP death and the item is unprotected, it will remain in the player's inventory; however, the item will be in a broken, unusable state and must be repaired by using it on [[Perdu]] and paying him {{NoCoins|96,000}} [[coins]]. The ornament is removed and Nigel '''cannot''' reapply it.{{fact}}

{{bug|
* The imbued ancient cape (Deadman) can't be stored in the [[cape rack]].
* The cape cannot be created by showing an imbued ancient cape to Nigel.}}
