The '''imbued ancient cape''' is a cosmetic variant of an [[God capes#Imbuing|imbued god cape]]. It is created by using an [[ancient fabric]], a reward from [[Pete Kayer]]'s final challenge, on one of the imbued god capes obtained from [[Mage Arena II]]. This process is irreversible. As a [[Zaros#Affiliated_items|Zarosian item]], it will grant [[god protection]] towards Zarosian NPCs in the [[God Wars Dungeon]].

If the player dies a PvP death and the item is unprotected, it will remain in the player's inventory; however, the item will be in a broken, unusable state and must be repaired by using it on [[Perdu]] and paying him {{NoCoins|96,000}} [[coins]].

The imbued ancient cape can be combined with the [[max cape]] to change its appearance by turning it into the [[imbued ancient max cape]].
