The '''Corrupted Scythe of Vitur''' is a melee weapon that requires level 60 [[Attack]] and 70 [[Strength]] to wield. Exclusive to [[Deadman Mode]] seasonals, it can be acquired by opening a [[trinket of advanced weaponry]] obtained from a [[Deadman:_Apocalypse#Breaches|breach]].

As a corrupted variant of the [[scythe of Vitur]], it has 20% lower bonuses than the regular variant, but only requires [[blood rune]]s to charge (300 per 100 charges). It also does not count as a scythe of Vitur for the [[Back in My Day...]] [[combat achievement]].
