The '''corrupted Tumeken's shadow''' is a magic weapon that requires level 75 [[Magic]] to wield. Exclusive to [[Deadman Mode]] seasonals, it can be acquired by opening a [[trinket of advanced weaponry]] obtained from a [[Deadman:_Apocalypse#Breaches|breach]].

As a corrupted variant of the [[tumeken's shadow]], the staff's magic attack bonus and [[magic damage]] are doubled rather than tripled from the player's worn equipment (within the [[Tombs of Amascut]], they are tripled rather than quadrupled).

{{Bug|If the player has charged the corrupted Tumeken's shadow and adds more charges, uncharging the staff will only return the latest batch of runes — e.g. charging 500 charges then 250 charges will only return 250 charges worth of runes when uncharged.}}

==Charging==
To use the staff's magic spell, it must be charged with [[soul rune|soul]] and [[chaos rune]]s, holding up to 20,000 charges when fully charged. Each cast requires 2 soul runes and 5 chaos runes. Players can configure the charges while the weapon is in their bank by right-clicking on Configure-Charges, as long as players have the required materials.
