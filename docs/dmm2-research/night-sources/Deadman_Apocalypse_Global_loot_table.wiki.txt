The global loot table for [[Deadman: Apocalypse]] is accessed by nearly all monsters killed during the game mode, except for [[raids]] and [[Nightmare Zone]].

==Rate of access==
{{DropLogProject|kills=3300000}}

The drop rate formulas shown below, together with the composition of the various subtables, were derived from [[RuneScape:Crowdsourcing|crowdsourced]] information during the previous [[Deadman: Reborn]] event, but have been confirmed to still apply to the Deadman: Apocalypse game mode.{{CiteTwitter|author=Mod Manked|url=https://twitter.com/Mankedupmage/status/1693247461089804303|date=20 August 2023|archiveurl=|archivedate=|quote=[Hey Manked, will the rate of hitting the global loot table in Apocalypse be the same as it was in Reborn? (...)] Supply drops are the same rates pretty much (adjusted sigil tier based on bracket to account for 5 brackets). Global loot table is the same (although it is "reworked" to be different items, so no longer 3rd age etc).}} Some differences exist: [[Deadman Mode#Global loot table|Ancient Warriors' weapons]] are now only dropped by monsters spawned from breaches, and items primarily obtained from [[Treasure Trails]] have been moved off the global loot table.

The chance of receiving supplies through the global loot table is halved when killing [[maniacal monkey]]s in [[Kruk's Dungeon]].{{CiteTwitter|author=Mod Manked|url=https://twitter.com/Mankedupmage/status/1692830703476441278|date=19 August 2023|archiveurl=|archivedate=|quote=[Can we expect to see any nerfs to MM2 tunnels? (...)] 50% nerf for supply drops in mm2 tunnels (...)}} [[Superior slayer monster]]s have a guaranteed chance of rolling on this table.

The rate of accessing this table depends on the combat level of the monster. The probability of rolling for this table after killing a monster with combat level <math>L</math> is approximately:

For monsters level 1 to 50:
:<math>\mathbb{P} \approx \frac{1}{250 + \left\lfloor (50 - L)^2/5 \right\rfloor}</math>
For monsters level 50 to 150:
:<math>\mathbb{P} \approx \frac{1}{\left\lfloor 337.5 - 1.75L \right\rfloor}</math>.
For monsters over level 150:
:<math>\mathbb{P} \approx \frac{1}{75}</math>

==Resources==
If the rolls for a sigil or emblem both fail, two rolls for resources are received.

The resource tables are broken up into multiple subtables of items that can be rolled for. Each item on all the subtables have the same chance to be rolled for. The roll depends on the monster's combat level using the formula: <math>\text{random}(\text{min}(\text{npcCombatLevel}, 100))</math>

===Emblems===
Tier 1 and tier 5 emblems have a chance to drop on any monster. The chance appears to be approximately 1/12 per resource drop, and you can only get one emblem per resource drop.

Monsters killed during [[Wilderness Slayer]] have additional chances to drop tier 1 emblems.
