==Charging the chronicle==
* {{tcond|If the chronicle is fully charged:}}
** {{mes|Your book is fully charged! It has 1,000 charges already.}}
** {{tact|end}}
* {{tcond|If charged with exactly one teleport card:}}
** {{tcond|If the chronicle was previously uncharged:}}
*** {{mes|You add a single charge to your book. It now has one charge.}}
*** {{tact|end}}
** {{mes|You add a single charge to your book. It now has [amount] charges.}}
* {{tcond|If charged with multiple teleport cards:}}
** {{mes|You add [amount] charges to your book. It now has [amount] charges.}}

==Teleporting==
* {{tcond|If the chronicle has no more charges:}}
** {{mes|Your book does not have any charges. Purchase some Teleport Cards from Diango.}}
** {{tact|end}}
* {{tcond|If the chronicle has no more charges after teleporting:}}
** {{qact|The player is teleported to the Champions' Guild}}
** {{mes|{{Colour|#ff0000|Your book has run out of charges.}}}}
** {{tact|end}}
* {{tcond|If the chronicle has exactly one charge remaining:}}
** {{qact|The player is teleported to the Champions' Guild}}
** {{mes|{{Colour|#ff0000|You have one charge left in your book.}}}}
** {{tact|end}}
* {{tcond|If the chronicle has multiple charges remaining:}}
** {{qact|The player is teleported to the Champions' Guild}}
** {{mes|{{Colour|#ff0000|You have [amount] charges left in your book.}}}}
** {{tact|end}}
