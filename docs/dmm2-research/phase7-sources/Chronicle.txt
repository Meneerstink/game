The '''Chronicle''' is an equipable book that can be used to [[Teleportation|teleport]] the player to just outside the [[Champions' Guild]], making it useful for players who plant seeds in [[Bush patches|bush]] and [[Hops patch|hops]] patches as well as free-to-play ironmen. The teleport is also a short distance from [[Charlie the Tramp]], making it useful for those doing [[Clue scroll (beginner)|beginner clues]]. 

During the [[2016 Easter event]], players were able to acquire it for free by defeating the [[Chocolate Chicken]] during the event. It can now be purchased from [[Diango's Toy Store]] for {{NoCoins|300|c}}. If the player has a copy of the Chronicle in the inventory or [[bank]], trying to buy another one from [[Diango]] will fail, and the user will receive the following message: ''"You've already got one of those."'' However, it '''is''' possible to own more than one Chronicle if buying them from [[Perdu]]. 

In order to use the Chronicle, it must be charged with [[teleport card]]s. Players can purchase them from [[Diango's Toy Store]] at a base price of {{NoCoins|150|c}} each. The book can hold a maximum of 1,000 charges, with one being used each time the Chronicle is used to teleport. Upon [[death]], when not an [[Items Kept on Death|item kept on death]], it is destroyed. If the Chronicle is lost or destroyed, it retains its charges if bought back.

The Chronicle's powers do not work in the [[Wilderness]].

==Trivia==
*This item was released to commemorate open beta access to Jagex's online card game ''{{RSL|Chronicle: RuneScape Legends|Chronicle: RuneScape Legends}}'', which was shut down in 2018. The item's name and examine text are references to that game.
*This is one of only three equipable books in F2P, the others being the [[Cruciferous codex]] and the [[Book of Egg]].
*The cards from Diango are 150 coins while [[Varrock teleport (tablet)|Varrock Teleport (tablet)]] varies in price on the GE and is typically more expensive; if you don't need to get to the GE but just to Varrock, consider using this to save a bit of money.
