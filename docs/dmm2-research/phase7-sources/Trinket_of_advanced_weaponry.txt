The '''trinket of advanced weaponry''' is an item in [[Deadman Mode]] that can be opened to give one of eight corrupted weapons, which are weaker versions of existing weapons. It cannot be opened if there is a player or obstacle in the way that would block the animation of opening it, and attempting to do so gives the message {{mes|You must have nothing surrounding you to open this trinket.}}.

[[File:Trinket of advanced weaponry opening.webm|thumb|left|250px|The animation of opening the trinket.]]
It can be acquired as a drop from monsters found during [[breach]]es, as well as from looting the [[Deadman's chest (scenery)|Deadman's chest]].
