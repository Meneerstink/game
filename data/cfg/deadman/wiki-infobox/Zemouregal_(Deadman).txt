'''Zemouregal''' is a monster that can appear at [[breach]]es in [[Deadman Mode]] and spawned alongside [[Deadman's chest (scenery)|Deadman's chests]] in [[Deadman: Annihilation]]. Wielding the [[Staff of Armadyl]], he attacks with magic and can summon [[Zemouregal Summon]]s. He utilises routefinding, meaning he cannot be [[safespot]]ted as he will path around obstacles to reach his target. He will also not stay focused on a single target, instead occasionally switching targets if other players are nearby.

When spawned from a breach, he is level 984; when defending a Deadman's chest, he is level 322. Only the breach versions drop the breach loot. The Deadman's chest version when killed will instead always drop a tier 5 [[Archaic_emblem#Tier_5|Archaic emblem]].

==Trivia==
* The config name of the breach version is <code>deadman_breach_lucien</code>, which suggests that it was originally intended to be [[Lucien]], rather than [[Zemouregal]]. This would also explain why Zemouregal is wielding the [[Staff of Armadyl]] here.
* Upon being defeated, he fades away into smoke, using the same animation that [[Mahjarrat]] do when appearing or disappearing.
