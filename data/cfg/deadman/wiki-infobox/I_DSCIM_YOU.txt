'''I DSCIM YOU''' is a monster which spawns from [[breach]]es in [[Deadman: Annihilation]]. It attacks with a [[dragon scimitar]] and is capable of using its special attack, which turns the player's [[protection prayers]] off. It will always use the special attack if its target isn't currently under the effect of the special attack. It also runs at its targets, rather than walking like most monsters.
