'''Pestilent Bloat''' is a monster which spawns from [[breach]]es in [[Deadman: Annihilation]]. It does not directly attack, but rather it wanders around, attacking players with range and line of sight with swarms of flies, which deal damage and display the message {{mes|Flies leave the body of the bloat and swarm you.}}. The flies can be completely protected against by praying [[Protect from Ranged]].
