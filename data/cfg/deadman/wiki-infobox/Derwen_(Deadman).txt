'''Derwen''' is a monster which spawns from [[breach]]es in both temporary and permanent [[Deadman Mode]]. He is completely immune to melee and ranged attacks, so he can only be damaged with magic attacks. It is also capable of casting [[Tele Block]] on players, preventing them from teleporting for 1 minute.
