'''Zemouregal Summons''' are summoned by [[Zemouregal (Deadman)|Zemouregal]] in [[Deadman: Annihilation]]. Both the breach version and [[Deadman's chest (scenery)|deadman's chest]] version of Zemouregal can spawn the summons. They are [[Aggressiveness|aggressive]] towards all nearby players and can be very dangerous to unaware players, as they can be lured even into safezones.

They utilise routefinding, meaning they cannot be [[safespot]]ted as they will path around obstacles to reach their target. They will also frequently switch targets if there are multiple players to target, and have a limited wander radius, so they can only chase players so far before they lose aggro.
