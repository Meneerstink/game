'''Dharok the Wretched''' is a monster which spawns from [[breach]]es in both temporary and permanent [[Deadman Mode]]. He is a much larger version of the regular [[Dharok the Wretched]], and has the same Wretched Strength mechanic which increases his max hit by % for every [[Hitpoint]] he is missing.
