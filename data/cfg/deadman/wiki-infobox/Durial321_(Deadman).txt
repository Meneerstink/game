'''Durial321''' is a monster which spawns from [[breach]]es in both temporary and permanent [[Deadman Mode]]. He attacks with both melee and magic, casting [[Ice Barrage]] on the player, which lasts for a shorter duration than regular [[Freeze|freezes]]. He is also capable of running after his target, unlike most NPCs.
